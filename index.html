What is Distributed Computing? 🤔

Imagine you want to find the most popular instagram tag among everyone at your school district, it could take you weeks to collect the results. But what if each classroom collected the results and then you combined them together? You'd be able to get the answer super quickly :)

That's distributed computing! Instead of one big computer doing all the work, many computers work together to solve huge problems, from predicting weather to powering your Google searches, building the next ChatGPT, or even deciding what TikTok videos to show you. Some of these problems are so hard that they can't be solved alone, and others are just way faster when the computers team up.
