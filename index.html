What is Distributed Computing? 🤔

Imagine you have a giant puzzle with 1,000,000 pieces. It would take you years to put it together alone! But what if you could share the pieces with thousands of friends all working at the same time?

That's distributed computing! Instead of one big computer doing all the work, many computers work together to solve huge problems — from predicting weather to running the internet — faster than any single machine ever could. ⚡
